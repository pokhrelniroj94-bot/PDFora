## Editorial cautions

- Official application requirements must be checked against the current issuing body's notice; generic blog references must not imply universal Loksewa image specifications.
- Gmail's 25 MB limit applies to personal Gmail accounts. Google Workspace sending/receiving limits can be administrator-configured.
- The reading-rate meta-analysis concerns adults reading English. It must not be presented as a measured Nepali reading-speed norm.
- PDF compression, conversion and repair outcomes depend on source-file structure and must not be guaranteed.
- The citations support the described concepts/workflows; they do not certify PDFora's own implementation or output quality.
