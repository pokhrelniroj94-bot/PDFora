## SEO acceptance criteria

- Keep generated/templated guide pages out of the index until they have independently useful, reviewed content.
- Preserve self-referencing canonical URLs for indexable pages and correct language alternates only where a genuinely localized equivalent exists.
- Keep unpublished locales and unreviewed localized tools out of sitemap and hreflang.
- Do not use word-count targets, FAQ markup or mass-generated pages as ranking shortcuts.
- Verify sitemap output, robots directives, metadata and rendered page content from the final built application.
- Treat Search Console crawl/index status as a post-deployment verification step; indexing and ranking are not guaranteed.
